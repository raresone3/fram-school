We are looking for an experienced Upper Primary class teacher to lead a Y4 to Y6 class at FRAM. You'll teach the Cambridge Primary curriculum across English, science, humanities and Philosophy for Children (P4C), with subject specialists handling music, art, PE, French and Romanian. Class sizes stay small and the same children stay with their class teacher across the upper primary years, so the pastoral relationship matters as much as the academic one.

The role starts in August 2026 for the 2026–2027 academic year.
