Dear families,

FRAM was born from a simple question: *what kind of school would I want for my own child?*

The answer was not only about strong academics, but about character, wellbeing and joy. I wanted a community where learning is rigorous but never cold, where values are lived rather than printed on a wall, and where every child feels that they truly belong.

At FRAM, we combine a solid academic foundation with a very human approach to education. Our three pillars, Academic, Welfare & Pastoral Care, and Skills & Events, work together so that children grow not only in knowledge, but also in confidence, resilience, empathy and independence. Lessons, clubs, events and the House System are all designed to help students discover who they are, what they care about and how they can contribute to the world around them.

I also believe strongly in partnership. A school does its best work when it walks side by side with families. If you decide to explore FRAM further, you will find an open door, honest communication and a team that genuinely wants to listen, adapt and grow together with you and your child.

Choosing a school is one of the most important decisions a family makes. My hope is that, as you get to know FRAM, you will feel the warmth, seriousness and purpose with which we do our work every day. And perhaps you will recognise in our vision something that aligns with your own hopes for your child's future.

I look forward to welcoming you to our campus and to sharing more of our story in person. Until then, feel free to contact us by any means and explore our website.

All the best,<br />
*Ileana Vrânceanu*
