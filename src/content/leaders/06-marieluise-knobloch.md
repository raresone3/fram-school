I strongly believe that providing children with a safe, nurturing environment, along with safeguarding and protecting their rights, is fundamental to their healthy development and ability to thrive.

My international training, received through the European Commission bodies and United Nations agencies I've worked alongside, attests to my deep commitment to supporting children's protection, safety and wellbeing.

As the Designated Safety Lead at FRAM School, my attention is on ensuring the safety and emotional support of all children, viewing their security as the essential foundation for their academic and social success. I am passionately dedicated to creating a secure environment where every child's rights are respected and their emotional needs are prioritised, independent of their country or culture of origin.

Leading the Welfare and Pastoral Care pillar, I work hand-in-hand with a team of experienced international professionals. Together, we are committed to building a community where children feel safe, valued, and able to reach their full potential.

I also believe that transparent, open communication with parents is key. I invite you to stay involved and contact us whenever needed, because trust and a sense of security are the backbone of a positive environment where children can truly flourish.

*Marieluise Knobloch*

For any safeguarding concern, write directly to safeguarding@framschool.com. Every disclosure or worry is treated seriously, recorded accurately, and handled by trained safeguarding staff in line with the school's policies.
