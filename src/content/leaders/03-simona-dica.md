At FRAM Primary School, teaching and learning are placed at the heart of all we do. The Academic team ensures that our curriculum, classroom practices, and assessments are delivered with consistency, quality, and care, always guided by the school's vision, mission, and values.

Teachers are supported in their professional growth, and learners are encouraged to develop curiosity, confidence, and a love for learning. Academic development is also closely linked with the work of the Welfare & Pastoral Care and Skills & Events teams, so that each child experiences a truly well-rounded education.

*Simona Dică*

Simona also teaches Y3D, which means academic standards at FRAM are set by a teacher still in the classroom every day, not from an office.
