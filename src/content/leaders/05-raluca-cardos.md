At FRAM we believe that the most profound learning happens when children experience the world firsthand. At the heart of what we do through the Yellow Pillar is a deep conviction: children learn best through doing, exploring, and experiencing.

We are passionate about creating opportunities that allow children to develop their skills and confidence through practical activities and meaningful experiences. What we want to achieve through our extracurricular activities is applying what we learn in Academics in real-life settings, as well as going deeper into our learning process by taking elements of theory and transporting these into practice.

For me, personally, seeing curiosity ignite and watching children grow through hands-on learning is the most inspiring and rewarding part of being an educator.

*Raluca Cardoș*

Raluca leads the Skills & Events Pillar, clubs, events, the House System, and the calendar that runs alongside academics, while also teaching Y4 and serving as the first staff leader of House Amelia Earhart.
