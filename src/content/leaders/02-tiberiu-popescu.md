Dear Families,

It is my pleasure to welcome you to FRAM School, a place where children learn, grow, and discover their unique potential in a warm, safe, and inspiring environment. Choosing the right school is one of the most important decisions a family can make, and at FRAM we approach this responsibility with deep care, professionalism, and genuine dedication.

Our vision is to guide students on a meaningful journey of learning and self-discovery. We are committed to nurturing not only strong academic foundations, but also the personal qualities that help children thrive in life: confidence, empathy, resilience, curiosity, and a sense of responsibility.

Every aspect of our school is shaped by our seven core values, Kindness, Curiosity, Awareness, Leadership, Fairness, Resilience, and Respect. These values are lived daily in our classrooms, in our community, and in the way we support children's emotional and social development.

As our campus continues to grow, our focus remains consistent: to provide a secure, structured, and stimulating environment where children feel seen, encouraged, and motivated to achieve their best. Our teachers, parents, and students form a strong and supportive partnership, united by the belief that education is a shared journey.

I invite you to explore our school, meet our team, and experience the positive, dynamic atmosphere that defines FRAM. We look forward to the opportunity to support your child's growth and to walk alongside your family throughout this important chapter. Please, do not hesitate to read through our website and contact us if you have any questions regarding our school, we are always happy to hear from you.

Warm regards,<br />
*Tiberiu Popescu*
